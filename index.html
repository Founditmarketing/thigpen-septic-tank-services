Who We Are

Since 1973, Thigpen Septic Tank Services has been providing reliable, professional septic solutions for homeowners and businesses alike. With over 52 years of experience, we have built a reputation for honesty, hard work, and exceptional customer service that has made us a trusted name in our community. As a family-owned, hometown company, we take pride in treating every customer like a neighbor, ensuring that every job is completed with care, precision, and attention to detail.

Our team handles a full range of septic services, including installations, repairs, maintenance, field drains, grease traps, and commercial systems. No matter the size or complexity of the project, we approach each task with the same dedication and professionalism, ensuring your system runs smoothly and efficiently. Regular maintenance and timely repairs help prevent costly issues and keep your home or business operating safely, and our knowledgeable technicians are always ready to provide guidance and support.

At Thigpen Septic, we understand the importance of trust and reliability. Every job we take on reflects our commitment to quality, integrity, and customer satisfaction. Whether it’s routine pumping, emergency repairs, or new system installations, our goal is to provide dependable septic solutions that protect your property, your investment, and your peace of mind. Serving our neighbors for more than five decades, we are proud to continue delivering the same level of dependable, professional service that has made Thigpen Septic Tank Services a cornerstone of our community.
